GAME MODES:

• Solo: Clear 10 levels by eating oranges before time runs out. Avoid stone obstacles and your own tail!

• CO-OP / Versus: 1-minute orange rush. Compete against another player for the highest score.

CONTROLS & MOVEMENT:

• Player 1 Movement: W (Up), A (Left), S (Down), D (Right) or Bottom D-Pad.

• Player 2 Movement: Arrow Keys or Top D-Pad in Co-op mode.

• Menu Navigation: Use Up/Down to select options, ENTER to confirm, and ESC to go back.
